### The five candidates I demoed (live, with real photos)

All vanilla CSS/JS, honour `prefers-reduced-motion`, port to WordPress cleanly.

1. **Infinite marquee** — duplicated row, `translateX(0 → -50%)`, pause on hover.
   Most literal "moving row"; risks reading as a startup template.
2. **Counter-scrolling double row** — two rows, opposite directions.
3. **Accordion** — `flex: 1 1 0` → `flex: 4 1 0` on hover/focus, name+role fade in.
   Closest to the Arup/WSP register; inert until interacted with.
4. **Cinematic stagger** — mask wipe + `scale(1.05 → 1)` +
   `grayscale(100% → 0)`, per-card delay. Closest to the existing reveal.
5. **Depth parallax** — per-card `translateX` at differing amplitudes/durations.
