# Impeccable — Design Context

> Updated 2026-09-02 (v3.3). Governs all design work in this project.
> Aesthetic: **Refined institutional**. Audience: private real-estate developers.
>
> **Hard delivery constraint (v3.3): every photo must be a plain `<img>` pointing
> at a `.jpg`.** No `<picture>`, no `<source type="image/webp">`, no `.webp`.
> The WorkBuddy preview server refuses `.webp` with a 404, and Chromium does
> **not** fall back to the `<img src>` when a matched `<source>` 404s — so a
> webp-first `<picture>` renders as an empty placeholder box. `.png` and `.jpg`
> are served fine. See "Asset delivery" below.
>
> **v2 was brutalist concrete and was rejected.** It was too harsh, too technical,
> too loud, and the palette/texture felt wrong. Do not reintroduce: zero radius,
> hard black rules, section numbering, dimension lines, monospace-everything,
> concrete grain texture, marquees, giant display type, or inverted harsh blocks.
>
> **Two things rejected in v3.2, per Ali — do not reintroduce:**
>
> - **Gold/brass eyebrow labels above section headings** («خدمات ما»,
>   «تجربه، تخصص و تعهد»). Deleted from all four pages. Section `h2`s now
>   stand alone; get hierarchy from scale and weight, not a kicker label.
> - **Decorative building imagery flanking the hero.** Tried as masked vertical
>   image strips at the left/right edges of the hero. Rejected outright.
>   The hero is centred copy on a quiet surface — keep it that way.

## Design Context

### Users

Private real-estate developers in Iran who are awarding design, supervision, or
project-management contracts for medium-to-large construction projects.

- **Context**: evaluating firms during a bidding / shortlisting window. Often on a
  desktop at work, frequently on a phone on a site visit. Under time pressure.
- **Job to be done**: decide, fast, whether این firm is *credible enough to shortlist*.
  Then find the one proof point that matters to their specific project type.
- **What proves credibility to them** (in order): delivered projects like theirs →
  years in operation → licence grade / ISO → who runs the firm → responsiveness.
- **Emotions the interface must evoke**: **confidence, solidity, competence.**
  Not delight, not warmth, not playfulness. They are risk-averse and spending
  serious money. Anything cute reads as unserious.

### Brand Personality

**Precise · Structural · Proven** (دقیق · استوار · مجرب)

- **Precise** — every number is specific and verifiable. No "بیش از صدها پروژه".
- **Structural** — the layout exposes its own logic. Visible rules, section
  numbering, hard edges. Nothing floats.
- **Proven** — track record leads. Claims are always adjacent to their evidence.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Refined institutional.** The Arup / WSP register: established, credible, calm.
The design should be *invisible* — content carries the page, not styling. A
developer evaluating this firm should feel steadiness and low risk, not
personality.

| Element | Decision |
|---|---|
| Radius | **Soft, never zero.** 3px inputs · 6px buttons/cards · 10–20px panels. |
| Depth | **Soft layered shadows**, subtle. Never heavy drop shadows. |
| Structure | **Quiet.** No section numbers, no dimension lines, no crop marks, no marquee. |
| Type | Vazirmatn 400/500/600/700. No monospace in the UI. Headings restrained — hero tops out at 3.5rem. |
| Texture | **None.** Flat warm surfaces only. No grain, no glassmorphism, no blur. |
| Layout | **Symmetric and centred.** Generous whitespace. Standard corporate rhythm. |
| Grouping | **Cards are correct here** — soft borders, subtle shadows, gentle hover lift. |
| Emphasis | Section `h2` alone — no eyebrow/kicker labels (deleted 2026-09-02). Never inverted harsh blocks except one rounded CTA panel. |
| Header | **Floating bar.** `position: sticky; top: 16px`, transparent wrapper, panel styling on `.site-header__inner` (20px radius, soft shadow that deepens on scroll). 16px side inset at every breakpoint so it never bleeds to the screen edge. |

**Colour system** (OKLCH, derived from the logo — not invented):

- **Warm stone neutrals** — chroma 0.003–0.014, hue ~85. Warmer and softer
  than the rejected concrete greys. Never pure grey or pure black.
- **Brand / primary** — the deep navy `#030351` that is **94.5% of the logo's
  pixels**, at `oklch(21% 0.125 274)`. Used for headings, primary buttons, footer.
- **Accent** — restrained brass `oklch(54% 0.11 68)`. Classic, credible,
  pairs with navy. **10% or less of visual weight** — eyebrows, stat suffixes,
  hover accents, credential years. Never a loud safety colour.

**Project hero art**: warm, minimal architectural *line illustrations* on the
subtle surface colour, stroked in soft navy (`--navy-400`). Thin strokes, soft
fills, a faint skyline, a ground line. **No** technical annotation, dimension
lines, crop marks, or code labels — that was the rejected v2 approach.

**Theme**: light default, dark toggle persisted to `localStorage`. Dark mode
redefines the semantic layer only (primitives never change): warmer dark surfaces
(not black), lifted accent, body weight 350.

### References & Anti-References

**References**: Arup, WSP, WSP Parsons Brinckerhoff, Foster + Partners,
Gensler. Established engineering and architecture consultancies.

**Anti-references — what this must explicitly NOT look like**:

- ❌ **Brutalist / raw concrete** — the rejected v2. No hard black rules, no zero radius, no section numbering, no concrete grain.
- ❌ Purple-blue gradients, cyan-on-dark, neon glows — the AI default palette.
- ❌ Glassmorphism / `backdrop-filter` blur panels.
- ❌ Inter / Roboto / Open Sans / Montserrat, or IRANSans for Persian.
- ❌ Monospace as a "technical" affectation.
- ❌ Giant display type (nothing above ~3.5rem), marquees, or asymmetric shouting.
- ❌ Bouncy / elastic easing.
- ❌ Stock-photo handshakes, hard-hats, or skyline silhouettes.
- ❌ Decorative image strips / masked photo panels flanking the hero or header (rejected v3.2).

### Design Principles

1. **Structure over decoration.** Every visual element either carries information
   or exposes structure. Ornament that does neither is deleted.
2. **Numbers over adjectives.** "۱۳۸۳" not "سال‌ها تجربه". "پایه یک" not "مجرب".
   Developers verify claims; give them something verifiable.
3. **Evidence adjacent to claim.** A credential is never stated without its
   issuing body and year next to it.
4. **Weight, not colour, for emphasis.** Hierarchy comes from scale, weight, and
   rules. Colour is reserved and rare — the orange means *act here*.
5. **RTL is the base case, not an afterthought.** Persian is the only language.
   Layout logic uses logical properties (`inline-start`, `padding-inline`); no
   `left`/`right`. Persian numerals throughout, tabular in mono.

### Constraints

- Persian-only, RTL, `lang="fa"`.
- Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS. No build step, no framework, no dependencies.
- Must work as a clickable demo *and* map 1:1 onto a future WordPress theme.
- WCAG AA minimum: 4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text and UI. `prefers-reduced-motion`
  honoured. 44px touch targets.
- Logo is low-res (265×212, 8 KB) — a vector replacement is needed before launch.

### Asset delivery (learned the hard way — v3.3)

- **Serve photos as `.jpg` via a plain `<img>`.** Never wrap them in
  `<picture>` with a `.webp` `<source>`.
  - The WorkBuddy preview server 404s `.webp` (verified: `ceo.webp` → 404 while
    `ceo.jpg` → 200 for files sitting in the same directory).
  - Chromium picks the first matching `<source>`; if **that** request fails it
    does **not** retry the `<img src>` fallback. Result: empty placeholder boxes
    that look like "images not loading".
  - This is invisible under `file://` (everything loads), so **always verify
    over the actual preview HTTP URL**, not `file://`.
- **Fonts are self-hosted** in `assets/fonts/` + `css/fonts.css`
  (Vazirmatn, 3 woff2 subsets, ~100 KB). The Google Fonts CDN is blocked by the
  preview server's CSP, so any `<link>` to `fonts.googleapis.com` silently fails
  and the whole site drops to a fallback face. Do not re-add the CDN link.
- Unreferenced `.webp` files still sit in `assets/team/` and `assets/projects/`.
  They are dead weight — safe to delete, kept only in case hosting later
  supports webp and we want to reinstate `<picture>`.
